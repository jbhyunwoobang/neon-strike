# 1. PROJECT VISUAL DNA — ONE PAGE

**Visual identity:** a civilization that poured its memory in concrete, drank the life out
of its land, and left the lights on. Monumental futurist-brutalism, worker-honest detail,
a world 90% grey discipline with its color spent in one deliberate place per frame.

**THE ONE-FRAME TEST (the identity in three tells):**
1. **THE POUR** — board-form concrete carrying its construction history (lift-lines,
   tie-holes, stains) at monumental scale.
2. **THE RATION** — ~90% grey restraint; the chromatic allowance spent once, on purpose.
3. **THE TENANT** — human presence under 3% of a space built for something larger.
*Passes all three = ours. Needs a logo = failed.*

**Emotional identity:** awe → smallness → unease → rationed shelter. Bigness reads as
*inheritance*, never triumph. Silence is visible. Hope is small and tended.
**Architectural identity:** the Gravity Style — mass as memory; engineered, load-honest,
over-provisioned, mostly empty.
**Color identity:** warm concrete greys; bone + amber = Edge; olive + one green line =
Sowers; paper + black = Ledger; living green ≤10% of frame, always earned; red =
Devastator/alarm only.
**Cinematic identity:** fixed lenses (24/35/50/85), one light source, headroom
oppression, stillness as style, space revealed before threat.
**Material identity:** wear is biography — every mark answers "what motion made this?";
maintenance is a budget line, and budgets are morality.

# 2. THE TEN ABSOLUTE RULES

1. **Architecture overpowers the player.** Humans <3% of frame height in establishing
   shots; insignificance staged at least once per 10 minutes, once per arena, at every
   chapter open.
2. **One dominant light source per space.** Everything else is bounce and shadow
   (40–60% of frame in readable shadow). Daylight = judgment; striplight = commerce.
3. **Green is currency.** ≤10% of any frame, always story-earned. Casual moss/foliage is
   a canon violation. Red belongs to Devastators and alarm only.
4. **Everything is engineered.** Visible load paths, counter-masses, drainage,
   maintenance access. If you can't sketch how it stands, it doesn't ship.
5. **Emptiness is intentional.** Dress the inhabited 5% with obsessive humanity; leave
   the built 95% disciplined. Vacancy reads as withdrawal, not laziness.
6. **Wear is biography.** Name the motion behind every scuff and the neglect behind
   every rust bleed. No decorative distress.
7. **The environment tells the story.** Every dressed space answers: who used this ·
   what did they do · when did it stop · what remains · *what was filed about it.*
   Paperwork is this world's blood-spatter.
8. **Nothing ornamental.** No decoration without function or history. The style's only
   rhetoric is repetition (pour forty columns where four suffice — metronomic).
9. **Two eras per surface.** The palimpsest law: wherever the camera can linger, show
   stratification — pour-lines, paint ghosts, wear paths, re-splices.
10. **No imports.** No neon, no chrome, no holograms, no glowing runes, no
    tactical-catalog gear, no other franchise's shorthand. When in doubt: worker-first
    honesty.

# 6. LIGHTING CHEAT SHEET

Natural: grey noon default (high overcast, shadowless dust-light). Direct sun ONLY via
monumental apertures (judgment), canopy chapters (alive), epilogue golden hour (once).
Belt nights: star-indecent `#2E3440`; city nights starless until the blackout.
Artificial: Edge = warm horizontal striplight, never overhead floods (downlights =
interrogation association only); industrial = sodium-amber pools, hard falloff,
gantry-mounted; emergency = amber layer pulsing at 12/min breathing tempo; religious =
lamplight `#E5B76A`, small radius, absolute; underground = cap-lamp/site-lantern beam
geometry. Two sources unavoidable? One dominates 4:1. Volumetrics: ONE idea per space;
god-rays only through story-bearing apertures. Fog: silhouette layering, 3 depth planes,
never gameplay-obscuring. Reflection: Edge civic floors mirror power; provincial never
reflects. Contrast is the rest state; flatness is alarm (Furrows are flat on purpose).
Boss schemes (locked): D-2 spotlight glare · Harrow smoke-dusk · D-3000 lobby blade +
red · Evergreen lit-from-below white→green. Cutscenes: lens-true, one source, no added
glamour lighting.

# 9. CAMERA LANGUAGE — ONE PAGE

Lenses: **24 / 35 / 50 / 85 spherical only. No zooms.** Gameplay FOV 70° (74° sprint).
Environmental reveals: approach compressed in 35 mm through thresholds → space given in
ONE 24 mm wide, held ≥4 s, player small. Never reveal a signature space in motion.
Bosses: **arena before boss** — space first, threat second. Close-ups: 50 mm, level,
patient; Grimwood alone gets formal symmetry + lit-from-below + heard-before-seen.
Asher: off-center, wall-adjacent, no dolly toward him before Ch.7. Voss: 85 mm through
doorframes. Negative space: weight above figures (headroom oppression = house style);
frame the architecture *between* speakers. Movement: dolly = procession; handheld =
inside grief only; lock-off = icons; crane = the two descents only. Shake ≤0.5°.
Every chapter: one ≥6 s silence shot — no music, no dialogue, no objective. The frame
must survive it.

# 10. GRAPHIC NOVEL — ONE PAGE

Two colors: ink `#2A2B2A` on paper `#D8D2C4` + green `#4F7A3D` ≤10%; red only in
Devastator panels ≤5%. Panels: 1–5/page, gutters ≥8% width; splash = full-bleed
architecture, tenant <3%. Ink: dry-brush structure over wash; hatching = machines only;
figures silhouette-flat; **one fully rendered face per sequence** (the page's spent
emotion). Type: small-caps serif captions ≤12 words; speech rare, hand-lettered;
Chargehand replaces captions after Ch.4. Motion: stroke-cluster assembly 200–400 ms;
parallax ≤4°; NO in-panel motion. Audio: paper texture + room tone + barest motif;
four empty bars precede every sequence (the Artist's signature — do not score them).
Transitions: last panel's dominant shape form-matches first gameplay frame. Dreams:
ink on black, no paper texture, the grey tide-rule advancing one page per act.
Flashbacks: aged darker, hand-stamped dates. Chapter splashes double as save-slot art.

# 11. UI — ONE PAGE

**Two diegetic hands:** Edge systems (grotesk, amber-on-dark, tract-serial grammar —
world screens + prologue HUD) vs. the Sower kit-bag (paper, Chargehand icons, hand-ruled
lines — player HUD after the defection; the HUD switch at Ch.1 IS a story beat).
HUD: health = thin double rule; stencil ammo counters; serif for chapter titles only;
sigh-response = brief amber vignette. **No minimap** — wayfinding is architectural; the
map screen is a hand-annotated compass-journal. Menus: journal spread (gear left, GN
memory thumbnails right); settings as equipment tags; **save = pressing a leaf.**
Lore: the Registry drawer — in-world facsimiles, never paraphrase. Subtitles:
paper-strip lower third; faction color = amber/green/paper, doubled by shape for
accessibility. Notifications: dry stamps, 120 ms, one bounce. Fonts: the two locked
families only; old-style numerals (Sower) vs tabular (Edge). Icons: Chargehand-derived,
2 px stroke @1080p, no gradients. Transitions: cuts and stamps, 150 ms; never slides.
Accessibility gates: shape-doubled color codes, 200% subtitles, amber pulse under
photosensitivity threshold, colorblind green→luminance variant (mandatory QA).

# 12. VFX — ONE PAGE

**One particle idea per frame.** Dust: wind-shadow wedges, aperture columns, hangs 20%
long. Fog: 3 silhouette planes, never obscures reads. Smoke: lit from within;
Ch.6 = amber-only law. Rain: wind-driven coastal events only; interiors leak on cue.
Water: mirror or violence, nothing between. Charge: *liquid light in conduit*
(teal-white Edge, green-white Sower) — flows, never arcs; no lightning bolts. **The
sigh:** outward desaturation wave, bark→canopy, leaves stilling; quietest effect in the
game (audio carries it). **Anti-sigh:** inverse dawn-flow; ≤10% green until Ch.10's
flood. Explosions: eased fireball + ground ring + soft crown; concrete spalls as plates
with rebar stubs — never gravel spray. Sparks: brief, warm, shop-honest. Ash: Ch.6–7
only. **Memory effects: none.** Memory is material (strata, paper, pages); a "memory
shader" is a canon violation. Fire: work-lit, structural, never decorative.

# 15. DO / DON'T

**DO:** stage monumental scale with a human tenant · spend green like money · one light
idea, one volumetric idea, one anomalous vertical · show load paths, drainage,
maintenance · dress the 5%, discipline the 95% · two eras per surface · let paperwork
tell the violence · make wear answer "what motion?" · reveal arenas before bosses ·

# FINAL PAGE — CREATIVE DIRECTOR CHECKLIST (25 QUESTIONS)

*An asset ships only on 25/25. "No" on 1–22 = revise. "Yes" on 23–25 = redesign.*

1. Does the frame pass the one-frame test (pour / ration / tenant)?
2. Is there exactly one dominant light source (or a documented 4:1 exception)?
3. Is green ≤10% of frame — and story-earned?
4. Is red absent (or justified by Devastator/alarm)?
5. Are humans <3% of the establishing view?
6. Can you sketch the load path of every structure shown?
7. Does every material's wear answer "what motion made this?"
8. Can you name the budget decision behind every patch of decay?
9. Does the space answer who / what / when / what remains / what was filed?
10. Is the inhabited 5% humanly dense and the rest disciplined?
11. Do at least two eras show on linger-able surfaces?
12. Are door/ceiling/pillar dimensions on the §3 table (or signed off)?
13. Is the district kit correct (materials, light, palette, props per §7)?
14. Is all signage in the correct voice (grotesk/serif/Chargehand)?
15. Is drainage/ventilation/maintenance access visible (unless this space *lies* by
    design — Voss's floor only)?
16. Is repetition metronomic — and any irregularity story-caused?
17. Is the shadow half of the frame still readable (`#1C1D1E`, not #000)?
18. Is motion rationed — would the shot survive a 6-second hold?
19. Are the lenses from the kit, negative space above the figures?
20. Do props come from (or match) the prop language — cab-forward, appliance-honest,
    over-provisioned?
21. Is the UI in the correct diegetic hand for the moment?
22. Does the asset serve the chapter's color script?
23. **Would this room/character/prop fit comfortably in another franchise?** → redesign.
24. **Does it need a logo to be recognized?** → redesign.
25. **Is anything here only because it looks cool?** → redesign.
